I've never really thought about wanting to be happy with the accomplishments that I have. Ever since the pandemic, I wanted to be something, someone, and achieve certain goals. A lot of those goals I didn't accomplish. I never went at the top of the stage during high school graduation. I never went to my dream college. I never did get perfect grades.
I really wanted to be on top with Jai on stage. Even if it wasn't a promise, years back I wanted to share my drive to work and help people. I really wanted to go to a good college because it meant I can help others more. Now I'm stuck living the fact that I'm always not enough. The fact of the matter stays: I hate myself.
Maybe it's all those pent-up feelings are what made me who I was in senior high school. Maybe it was just me settling in with someone who actually "love" me for who I am. I don't know if what I'll do or think with those "mistakes" of the past that still lingers with me or will it just go away on its own as I settle down in life.
I can NEVER love anyone. At least not true love. I now realize that no one can love anyone if they don't love themselves. I can crush or be infatuated with someone, yes, but I can never really love someone. True love comes from the heart and self-sacrifice, not self-giving.
The future freaks me out. Maybe I'll barely pass my classes and "just" graduate with a degree in computer engineering and get a minimum wage job at a cell-phone repair job or a cashier in Jollibee. I still want to care for my future family. I'll try my best.
